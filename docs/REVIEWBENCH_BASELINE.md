## Gold reconciliation

The imported dataset contains the 352 findings labeled TP, not the complete labeled gold table. At the pinned ReviewBench revision, the official 25-case findings contain 565 labeled records: 352 TP and 213 FP. An exact tuple comparison of the extracted TP records against the published findings matched all 352 records with zero mismatches. The compared fields were path, line range, message, severity, category, scope, difficulty, and context requirement.

The full-gold-set digest above is SHA-256 over newline-terminated, case-ID-sorted lines in the form `<case_id> <sha256-of-pinned-golden-file>`. The machine-readable per-case reconciliation is committed as [gold-reconciliation.json](benchmark-artifacts/reviewbench-25/gold-reconciliation.json).

The official methodology says the corpus labels were first generated by a classifier, then human-audited and corrected; it reports 47 false TP labels corrected and 96.6% TP/FP agreement. The published methodology identifies the TP labels as human-reviewed ground truth, but the extracted dataset does not carry per-finding adjudication history. We can verify exact equality to the published gold, not independently reproduce each human decision. See the [methodology pinned to the source revision](https://github.com/review-bench/ReviewBench/blob/12e9d1aadbe7ddf4a480c6bd4ffb6ae37c7bcb67/docs/METHODOLOGY.md).
